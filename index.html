Psychard

Small games, mostly for phones.

Games

  • Office Hop Clear a never-ending to-do list that gets more ridiculous the longer you last.
  • Climb Drag a stick figure's hands and feet up a bouldering wall before your stamina runs out.
  • Scale Tower Practise violin scales, scored on how well you play in tune.
  • Cycle Blaster A shooter about surviving chemotherapy.
  • Tritave Sequence music in the Bohlen-Pierce scale, built on the tritave instead of the octave.
